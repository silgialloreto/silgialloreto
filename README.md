## 🔬 Projects

### Thesis
**[ODEP & Deep Learning – label-free stratification of prostate cancer cells](https://github.com/silgialloreto/odep-deep-learning-cell-stratification)**  
Experimental M.Sc. thesis covering the whole R&D pipeline:
- design and fabrication of a dual-channel Lab-on-a-Chip with a sandwich architecture (PMMA, ITO glass, a-Si:H);
- ODEP measurements on LNCaP, DU145 and PC3 cells under a Leica DMi8 microscope;
- a Python pipeline (Cellpose, R(2+1)D video network) that learns cell morphology and defocus dynamics.

The dynamic R(2+1)D model reaches **83% balanced accuracy**, against 78% for a 2D CNN and 62% for LDA.
